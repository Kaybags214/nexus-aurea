# Check 3 — Pieces, weight and charges

- [ ] Number of pieces / RCP stated
- [ ] Gross weight stated **with its unit** — kg or lb must be explicit
- [ ] Rate class shown
- [ ] Chargeable weight present
- [ ] Chargeable weight is not less than gross weight
- [ ] Piece count agrees with the packing list

## Chargeable weight

Chargeable weight is the greater of actual gross weight and volumetric weight. A chargeable
weight **below** gross weight is arithmetically impossible and is Critical — it means one of
the two was mis-entered, and the shipment is being under-charged and under-declared.

## When the piece count disagrees with the packing list

Per `standards-of-precedence.md` section 4, the physical count governs, then the AWB, then the
packing list. The finding is normally "packing list overstates piece count — correct to n",
not the reverse.

If the physical count is unknown, say so — do not assume the AWB is right just because it
outranks the packing list on paper.

## Unit mixing

A shipment with gross weight in kg and dry ice weight in lb is a finding even if both are
internally correct. Mixed units on one document cause acceptance errors.
