# Check 3 — Dates, retest period and shelf life

- [ ] Date of manufacture
- [ ] Date of analysis / release
- [ ] Expiry date or retest date, clearly labelled as which
- [ ] Certificate issue date

## Arithmetic to run

- [ ] Date of analysis is not earlier than date of manufacture
- [ ] Certificate issue date is not earlier than date of analysis
- [ ] Expiry / retest date is in the future relative to the shipment date
- [ ] Remaining shelf life at delivery meets any customer or contractual minimum

## Findings

- **Expired lot in transit** → Critical
- Expiry date passing during a foreseeable transit or quarantine → Critical
- Retest date labelled as expiry, or the reverse → Major; they are different commitments
- Any date sequence that runs backwards → Major, and it may indicate a fabricated certificate

## Remaining shelf life

Many pharma contracts require a minimum remaining shelf life on delivery — often expressed as a
percentage or a fixed number of months. **Do not assume a figure.** If a quality agreement or
purchase order states one, check against it and cite it. If none is available, state the
remaining shelf life as a fact and note that no minimum was available to check against.
