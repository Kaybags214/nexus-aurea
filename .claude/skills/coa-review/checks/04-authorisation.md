# Check 4 — Authorisation and document integrity

- [ ] Signed or approved by a named person
- [ ] Title or role of that person shown, and it is a QC / QA authority
- [ ] Date of signature
- [ ] Company letterhead or identifiable issuer
- [ ] Certificate or document number, and page **n of n** where multi-page

## Findings

- **Unsigned certificate** → Critical. An unsigned CoA is a draft
- Signature present but signatory unnamed or untitled → Major
- Missing page in a multi-page certificate → Critical; a test could be on the absent page

## Integrity

- [ ] No alteration, overwriting or correction fluid on results, limits or dates
- [ ] Where the CoA is a scan or photo, it is a complete page, not a crop

An altered result field is Critical and is not correctable in the report — the finding is that
an authentic replacement certificate is required from the manufacturer.

## Legibility

Any result, limit, date or signature you cannot resolve from the image is
**"cannot verify from image"** and a reshoot request. Name the exact fields. Do not infer a
value from context, and do not treat an unreadable result as passing.
