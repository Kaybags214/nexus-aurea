# Check 5 — Form condition

- [ ] Printed with the required red hatched border on both vertical edges
- [ ] Legible, in English, plus any additional language the states require
- [ ] **No erasures or overwriting**
- [ ] Sufficient copies provided for the operator

## Alterations

Amendments must be made and signed by the same person who signed the declaration.

Anything scratched out and written over is **Critical**, and the fix is a fresh form — not a
correction in the report. Do not produce a "corrected version" of an altered DGD as though the
alteration were repairable; state that a clean replacement is required.

## The red hatched border

A declaration printed without it — a plain photocopy, a screenshot, a generic template — is
Critical. The border is how acceptance identifies the document at a glance.

## Legibility from a photograph

If the border, a signature or a classification field cannot be resolved from the image, that is
**"cannot verify from image"** and a reshoot request, not a finding of absence. Say precisely
which fields are unreadable.
