## Why every row here is Critical

The AWB is the transport contract and the DGD is the safety declaration. Where they disagree,
the shipment is simultaneously two different shipments on paper. There is no minor version of
that.

## Resolving

The DGD outranks the AWB for anything hazmat-related; the physical package outranks both.
Name which document is wrong and what it should say.

## When no AWB was submitted

Say so and mark this check not performed. A DGD reviewed alone has not been verified against
the transport document, and the report must not imply otherwise.
