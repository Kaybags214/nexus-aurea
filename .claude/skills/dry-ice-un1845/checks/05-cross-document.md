## Resolving a disagreement

Do not average, pick the larger, or silently normalise. Per `standards-of-precedence.md`
section 4, the order is: the physical package, then the DGD, then the AWB, then the invoice,
then the packing list.

Name which document is wrong and what it should say. The finding is the mismatch itself.
