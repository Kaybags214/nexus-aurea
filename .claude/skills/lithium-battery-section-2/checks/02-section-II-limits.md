# Check 2 — Section II eligibility

Section II is a **relief from the full requirements**, available only to smaller cells and
batteries. Qualification is not a judgement call; it is a set of thresholds.

## What has to be established

- [ ] **Watt-hour rating per cell** (lithium ion) — marked on the cell or in the specification
- [ ] **Watt-hour rating per battery** (lithium ion)
- [ ] **Lithium content in grams per cell** (lithium metal)
- [ ] **Lithium content per battery** (lithium metal)
- [ ] **Number of cells or batteries per package**
- [ ] **Net quantity of cells/batteries per package**
- [ ] Where equipment is involved, the **number of batteries installed per device**

## Do not supply the thresholds from memory

Watt-hour ceilings, lithium-content limits and per-package limits are among the most frequently
revised figures in the DGR, and they differ per packing instruction and per section.

**Verify each against the operator's own 67th ed. copy.** Where no copy is available, record
every threshold you could not confirm in the sidecar `unverified` array and state plainly that
Section II eligibility was **not established**.

## Not established means not eligible

Section II is a relief that a shipment must earn. If the document does not carry the watt-hour
rating or lithium content, eligibility cannot be established — and the finding is that the
shipment does not qualify on the evidence presented, not that it probably does.

This is the opposite of the usual reading. Elsewhere an absent field is a documentation defect;
here it removes the basis for the relief being claimed.

## Damaged, defective or recalled

- [ ] Any indication the cells are damaged, defective or subject to recall

These are forbidden from air transport under the ordinary packing instructions and cannot ride
under Section II at all. Any such indication is **Critical** and stops the review.
