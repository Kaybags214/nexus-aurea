# Check 3 — State of charge and aircraft limitation

## State of charge

Lithium **ion** cells and batteries shipped **alone** carry a state-of-charge restriction.

- [ ] State of charge stated for UN3480 shipments
- [ ] The stated figure is within the limit — **verify the limit against the DGR 67th ed.**
- [ ] Where the shipment is UN3481 (with or in equipment), note whether the restriction applies

An absent state of charge on a UN3480 shipment is a finding: the restriction cannot be shown to
be met.

## Aircraft limitation — the one that gets shipments pulled

- [ ] Aircraft limitation stated on the declaration or handling information
- [ ] It matches what the packing instruction and section permit
- [ ] The AWB carries the same limitation as the declaration

**Lithium ion and lithium metal cells and batteries shipped alone carry passenger-aircraft
restrictions that batteries in equipment do not.** Confirm the current rule for the specific UN
number and section against the DGR — do not assume from the commodity alone.

Where a shipment is restricted to cargo aircraft:

- [ ] The **Cargo Aircraft Only label** is required on the package
- [ ] The AWB handling information says cargo aircraft only

## A contradiction between documents is Critical

Where the declaration and the AWB disagree on aircraft limitation, that is **Critical** and it
is not a clerical matter. Acceptance reads the AWB. A package barred from passenger aircraft can
be loaded onto one on the strength of an AWB that does not say otherwise.

Name both documents and both values in the finding.
