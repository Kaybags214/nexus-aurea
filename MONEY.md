## Rules

- **IRS primary sources only.** `irs-source-links.md` is the source list; a forum answer is not
  a source.
- These are **learning notes, not filed positions.** Nothing here is tax advice, and the CPA
  decides. `cpa-questions.md` is where uncertainty goes — not into a note written as fact.
- Deadlines drive routines. If `tax-deadlines.md` changes, the deadline routine changes with it.
