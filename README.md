# Nexus Aurea Command Center

Purpose: Central documentation system for Nexus Aurea Inc. business infrastructure, logistics compliance, tax learning, market research, resume development, and proof-of-work.

## Main Lanes

### Compliance

Regulatory references, SOP drafts, training notes, audit notes, and forms.

### Dangerous Goods / DGR

IATA DGR, 49 CFR, shipper declarations, lithium battery references, checklists, and practice files.

### Cold Chain

GDP/GMP, temperature-control documentation, dry ice, IoT monitoring notes, excursions, and lane-risk review.

### Customs

CBLE study, commercial invoice notes, HS code research, entry documents, import/export basics, and practice files.

### Templates

Air Waybill, Shipper's Declaration, Commercial Invoice, Packing List, SOP templates, and checklist templates.

### Tax Infrastructure

Form 1120 learning, bookkeeping, CPA questions, tax deadlines, IRS source links, and state-tax notes.

### Market Watch

Public-company research through filings, fundamentals, risks, ownership, dilution, management changes, and infrastructure value.

### Resume

Master resume and targeted resumes for logistics compliance, dispatcher, and trade compliance roles.

### Audit Lab

Proof-of-work folder for document review practice, discrepancy findings, dry ice / UN1845 checks, lithium battery review, commercial invoice review, AWB review, and cold-chain excursion practice.

## Operating Rule

Build the system before chasing the opportunity.
