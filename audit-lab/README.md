# Audit Lab

Purpose: Practice document review and compliance findings for air cargo, dangerous goods, customs, and cold-chain shipments.

## Audit Areas

- Air Waybill review
- Shipper's Declaration review
- Commercial Invoice review
- Packing List review
- Dry Ice / UN1845 documentation
- Lithium battery documentation
- Cold-chain excursion review
- Sample discrepancy findings

## Review Focus

- Proper names and addresses
- UN numbers
- Proper shipping names
- hazard class / division
- packing group, if applicable
- net quantity
- dry ice weight in kg
- temperature range
- emergency contact information
- shipper and consignee consistency
- invoice and AWB consistency
- declared value / customs information
- signatures and dates

## Rule

This folder is for training, practice, and proof-of-work. Do not store confidential customer documents here unless they are redacted.
