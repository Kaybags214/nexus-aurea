# Dry Ice / UN1845 Review Checklist

Purpose: Review air cargo documentation for shipments containing dry ice used as a refrigerant.

## Critical Checks

- UN1845 is shown where required
- Proper shipping name: Dry ice or Carbon dioxide, solid
- Class 9 marking/labeling is addressed where required
- Net weight of dry ice is shown in kilograms
- Air Waybill handling information includes dry ice details
- Package count matches shipment documents
- Shipper and consignee match across documents
- Temperature-control instructions are present if applicable
- Packaging appears suitable for venting carbon dioxide gas
- No conflicting dry ice weights appear across documents

## High-Risk Errors

- Missing dry ice net weight in kg
- Dry ice weight shown in pounds only
- UN1845 missing from AWB or related document
- Temperature range missing for temperature-controlled product
- Shipper/consignee mismatch
- Dry ice shown on one document but missing from another
- Package count mismatch
- No handling instruction for refrigerated shipment

## Recommended Fixes

- Add dry ice net weight in kg
- Correct UN1845 and proper shipping name
- Align shipper and consignee across all documents
- Align package count across AWB, invoice, packing list, and declaration
- Add temperature-control handling instructions
- Confirm dry ice quantity before tendering shipment
