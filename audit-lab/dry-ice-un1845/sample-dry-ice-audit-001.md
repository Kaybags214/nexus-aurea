# Sample Dry Ice Audit 001

## Scenario

A temperature-controlled pharmaceutical shipment is moving by air with dry ice used as a refrigerant.

## Document Set Reviewed

- Air Waybill
- Commercial Invoice
- Packing List
- Dry Ice / UN1845 handling information

## Shipment Summary

- Commodity: Temperature-controlled pharmaceutical product
- Refrigerant: Dry Ice / UN1845
- Mode: Air
- Temperature requirement: 2°C to 8°C
- Origin: United States
- Destination: International

## Review Findings

### Critical Checks

- UN1845 should be shown where required.
- Proper shipping name should be Dry ice or Carbon dioxide, solid.
- Net weight of dry ice must be shown in kilograms.
- Air Waybill handling information should identify dry ice details.
- Shipper and consignee should match across all documents.
- Package count should match across AWB, invoice, and packing list.
- Temperature-control instructions should be present.

### Potential Discrepancies

- Dry ice weight missing from AWB.
- Dry ice weight shown in pounds only instead of kilograms.
- Temperature range not shown on handling instructions.
- Package count differs between invoice and packing list.
- Shipper address does not match across documents.

## Recommended Corrections

- Add dry ice net weight in kilograms.
- Confirm UN1845 and proper shipping name.
- Align package count across all documents.
- Add temperature-control handling instruction.
- Confirm shipper and consignee details before tender.

## Final Review Status

Hold for correction before tendering shipment.

## Training Note

This sample is for practice and proof-of-work only. It is not based on a confidential customer shipment.
