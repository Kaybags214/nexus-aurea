### 🔴 Critical findings

- **[C-1] The Air Waybill number is ten digits.** Written `771-221-2765` on all three documents.
  An AWB is **eleven** digits: a 3-digit airline prefix plus an 8-digit serial whose last digit
  is a check digit. One digit is missing.
  If the serial is `2212765`, the arithmetic gives `2212765 ÷ 7 = 316109 remainder 2`, so the
  check digit is **2** and the full number would be `771-2212 7652`.
  **Standard:** IATA Resolution 600a; `templates/01-awb/awb-reference-template.md`.
  **Fix:** obtain the complete eleven-digit number from the carrier. Do not append a digit on
  the strength of this arithmetic alone — it assumes the seven digits shown are the serial.

- **[C-2] The Air Waybill gross weight column contains a volume.** The weight fields read
  `3.0 kg` and `1.0 L`. Litres are volume, not weight, and cannot sit in a gross weight column.
  Separately, `3.0 kg` equals the dry ice **net** weight exactly, so it cannot also be the
  shipment gross weight — packaging and the biological substance are unaccounted for.
  **Fix:** weigh the consignment and enter a true gross weight in kg.

### 🟡 Major findings

- **[M-1] Packing group "II" on UN1845.** Dry ice has no packing group.
  **Standard:** `dgr/03-shipper-declarations/completed-example-reference.md`. **Fix:** leave blank.
- **[M-2] Packing group "I" on the UN3373 entry.** Category B under PI 650 carries no packing
  group. *Confirm against a DGR copy.* **Fix:** leave blank.
  *(M-1 and M-2 repeat from the typed version of this shipment — see the pattern section.)*
- **[M-3] Emergency contact is malformed.** Written `703-773-8318-8319` — this reads as two
  numbers run together, and carries no country code. **Fix:** one number, international format.
- **[M-4] Place of signing absent.** Date present, place not.
- **[M-5] Proper shipping names deviate.** "Carbon Dioxide (solid)" — the DGR name is
  "Carbon dioxide, solid" or "Dry ice". "Biological Substances Category B" — the name is
  "Biological substance, Category B", singular.
- **[M-6] Packaging type not specified.** "2 Packauing" (sic) gives a count, not a packaging
  type or specification code. The UN3373 entry says "2 glass Container" — glass is the inner
  receptacle, not the outer packaging, and PI 650 requires triple packaging, which is not
  described. **Fix:** name the outer packaging.
- **[M-7] A Shipper's Declaration is not required for this shipment.** Category B under PI 650
  requires none, and dry ice as a refrigerant requires none. **Standard:**
  `pharma/03-biological-substances/biological-substances-reference.md` Step 3.
  As practice this is valid; as a real tender it would be an over-declaration.
