# CBP Entry Forms — Reference (Form 7501 and Form 3461)

Purpose: Summary of the two core U.S. Customs and Border Protection entry forms relevant to import/export documentation practice. These are public-domain U.S. government forms — download the official PDFs directly from cbp.gov using the links below.

## CBP Form 7501 — Entry Summary

- Purpose: Identifies merchandise entering U.S. commerce and documents duty/tax owed. Used by CBP for classification, valuation, origin determination, and to supply statistical data to the U.S. Census Bureau.
- Filed by: Importer or importer's agent, for each import transaction.
- Timing: Must be filed within 10 working days from the time of entry of merchandise into the United States.
- Regulatory basis: 19 CFR Part 102 and related sections.
- Key data fields: entry number, entry date, importer of record, consignee, HS/HTSUS classification per line, country of origin, entered value, duty rate, duty/tax/fee amounts, surety bond information.

## CBP Form 3461 — Entry/Immediate Delivery

- Purpose: Application for entry/immediate delivery, certifying the submitted information is accurate, the bond is sufficient and valid, and requirements of 19 CFR Part 142 are met.
- Use: Source document allowing a CBP officer to verify consignee and shipment information and confirm a bond is on file before cargo release.
- Key data fields: importer of record number, entry type, carrier code, vessel/flight and arrival date, port of entry, bill of lading/AWB number, manifest quantity, and description of merchandise.

## Review / Practice Checklist

- Does the HS/HTSUS classification on 7501 match the commercial invoice description?
- Does the entered value match the commercial invoice total plus any dutiable additions (freight/insurance, per the applicable valuation method)?
- Does the country of origin on 7501 match the certificate of origin or invoice?
- Does the bill of lading/AWB number on 3461 match the actual transport document?
- Was 7501 filed within the 10-working-day window from entry?
