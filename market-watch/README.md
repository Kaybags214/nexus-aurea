# Market Watch

Purpose: Track selected public companies through filings, fundamentals, risks, ownership, and long-term infrastructure value.

## Current Watchlist

The authoritative lists live in:

- `watchlist.md` - equities, ETFs, Shenzhen-listed optical names, private-company signals
- `blockchain-pharma-watchlist.md` - crypto networks and tokens studied for pharma/logistics use

## Daily Update

An automated update runs Monday through Friday at 4:30 PM ET, 30 minutes after the U.S. equity
close, and writes `screener-reports/report_YYYY-MM-DD.html`.

`daily-update-playbook.md` defines what each run must cover: market data, fundamentals, analyst and
ownership data, news, full SEC filing review (10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, DEF 14A, Form 4, Form 144, S-1/S-3/424B,
13D/13G/13F), crypto market and network activity, and private-company signals.

## What This Folder Tracks

- SEC filings
- Company notes
- Sector notes
- Filing review checklists
- Ownership and dilution
- Debt and liquidity
- Management changes
- Regulatory issues
- Business model changes
- Long-term infrastructure value

## Rule

This folder is for research and education. It is not a trading journal and not financial advice.

Price movement matters, but filings matter more.
