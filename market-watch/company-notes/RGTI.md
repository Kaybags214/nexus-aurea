**Read-through:** GAAP net income/loss for RGTI is currently dominated by warrant-liability revaluation, which moves with the stock price (stock up → liability value up → non-cash "loss"; stock down → non-cash "gain"). Track non-GAAP loss and cash burn rate, not the GAAP headline, to judge actual business trajectory. Revenue is still small (single-digit millions/quarter) against ~$85M/year of opex — the company is pre-commercial-scale and funded almost entirely by its $569–590M cash pile plus periodic equity/strategic raises.

## Companies It Owns / Is In Business With

### Subsidiary
- **Rigetti UK Limited** — wholly owned subsidiary (UK Companies House-registered). Runs Rigetti's UK quantum computer at the National Quantum Computing Centre; won Innovate UK's Quantum Missions Pilot Competition to advance error-correction work.

### Strategic investor / commercial partner
- **Quanta Computer Inc.** (Taiwan, Fortune 500 server/notebook manufacturer, ~$43B annual sales) — closed a **$35M equity investment** in Rigetti on 2025-04-29 (~$11.59/share). Both companies committed to invest **$100M+ each over 5 years** into a joint superconducting quantum computing development/commercialization effort. This is Rigetti's most important strategic relationship: Quanta brings manufacturing scale, Rigetti brings the quantum IP. Watch for: follow-on investment tranches, joint product announcements, whether the $100M mutual commitment is being met on schedule.

### Technology/integration partners (not equity relationships)
- **Riverlane Ltd** (UK) — quantum error-correction stack integrated into Rigetti's 36-qubit UK system at the National Quantum Computing Centre.
- **QphoX** (Netherlands, startup) — collaborating with Rigetti on a $5.8M AFRL contract for superconducting quantum networking (frequency-conversion tech).
- **QuantrolOx** — control-automation hardware/software integrated into the system Rigetti shipped to the University of Saskatchewan (March 2026).

### Government / research customers and grant partners
- **DARPA** — awarded the IMPAQT contract for quantum algorithms targeting combinatorial optimization problems.
- **AFRL (Air Force Research Laboratory)** — $5.8M, 3-year contract (with QphoX) for quantum networking.
- **NASA / USRA** — hybrid quantum-classical algorithm work for binary optimization problems.
- **Department of Energy (DOE)** — named as a government partner/customer in filings.
- **India's C-DAC** (Centre for Development of Advanced Computing) — international government customer.

## Comparison Note vs. QBTS (D-Wave)

Both RGTI and D-Wave are running the same playbook right now: small quarterly revenue ($2-7M range), large non-cash GAAP swings (warrants for both), $500-600M+ cash piles from 2025 capital raises, and government-contract-driven credibility rather than commercial-scale revenue. Key difference: D-Wave's government relationship escalated to direct federal equity ownership (CHIPS Act, Dept. of Commerce), while Rigetti's most significant outside equity relationship is private-sector (Quanta Computer) rather than governmental. D-Wave is pursuing a hybrid annealing + gate-model strategy (via the Quantum Circuits Inc. acquisition); Rigetti is pure gate-model superconducting, betting on qubit-count/fidelity roadmap execution instead of an acquisition-driven pivot.
