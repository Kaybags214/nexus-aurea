## Filing Review Checklist

### 8-K

- What happened?
- Is it material?
- Does it involve management, debt, acquisition, financing, resignation, litigation, delisting risk, or capital structure?
- Does it change the thesis?

### 10-Q

- Revenue trend
- Net income or loss
- Cash position
- Debt
- Dilution or share count
- Segment performance
- Risk factor changes

### 10-K

- Business model
- Major risks
- Regulatory exposure
- Debt and liquidity
- Management discussion
- Ownership structure
- Long-term strategic position

### DEF 14A

- Board structure
- Executive compensation
- Shareholder proposals
- Insider ownership
- Voting control

### Form 4

- Insider buying
- Insider selling
- Option grants
- Pattern of transactions
