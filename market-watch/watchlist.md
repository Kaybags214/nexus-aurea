## Filing Types to Track

- 8-K: Major corporate events
- 10-Q: Quarterly report
- 10-K: Annual report
- DEF 14A: Proxy statement, governance, executive compensation
- Form 4: Insider transactions
- S-1: Registration statement / IPO filing
- S-3: Shelf registration
- 424B: Prospectus supplement / offering detail

## Review Rules

- Do not rely on price alone.
- Read filings before forming opinions.
- Track dilution, debt, cash flow, management changes, risk factors, and business model changes.
- Separate speculation from evidence.
- Watch ownership changes, share issuance, capital raises, and regulatory issues.
- Treat hype sectors carefully.
