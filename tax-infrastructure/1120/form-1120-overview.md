# Form 1120 Overview

Purpose: Learn how a C corporation reports income, deductions, credits, and tax liability on Form 1120.

## Core Rule

Form 1120 is the federal income tax return for a C corporation. It summarizes the corporation's income, deductions, taxable income, tax, payments, and required schedules.

## What Nexus Aurea Must Understand

- Was there revenue?
- Were there deductible business expenses?
- Were any expenses startup or organizational costs?
- Were any expenses personal and therefore not deductible?
- Were any assets purchased that need depreciation?
- Were there officer payments or reimbursements?
- Were state corporate filings required?
- Were bookkeeping records complete enough to support the return?

## Main Learning Areas

1. Income
2. Deductions

4. Payments and credits
