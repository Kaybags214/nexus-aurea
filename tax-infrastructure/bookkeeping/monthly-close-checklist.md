# Monthly Close Checklist

Purpose: Build a monthly habit for keeping Nexus Aurea Inc. books clean before tax time.

## Monthly Review

- Review bank transactions
- Separate business and personal expenses
- Save receipts and invoices
- Record income, if any
- Record business expenses
- Check subscriptions and recurring charges
- Review mileage or travel records
- Review equipment purchases
- Note any reimbursable expenses
- Update CPA questions

## Expense Categories to Track

- Bank fees
- Software and subscriptions
- Training and certifications
- Business licenses and registrations
- Office supplies
- Equipment and technology
- Website and domain costs
- Professional services
- Travel and mileage
- Postage and shipping
- Marketing and advertising
- Meals, if business-related and documented

## Monthly Questions

- Did the company earn revenue this month?
- Did I pay for anything personally that belongs to the business?
- Did the company buy equipment that may need depreciation?
- Are all receipts saved?
- Are all transactions explained?
- Is anything unclear for the CPA?

## Rule

Do not wait until tax season to organize records. Monthly cleanup prevents year-end mistakes.
