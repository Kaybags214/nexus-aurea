# Tax Deadlines

Purpose: Track federal, state, and internal tax deadlines for Nexus Aurea Inc.

## Federal C Corporation Deadlines

- Form 1120 is generally due by the 15th day of the 4th month after the end of the corporation's tax year.
- For a calendar-year C corporation, that generally means April 15.
- Extensions may give more time to file, but not more time to pay.

## Internal Monthly Deadline

By the 5th of each month:

- Review prior-month bank transactions
- Save receipts
- Update bookkeeping notes
- Add CPA questions
- Review any business expenses paid personally
- Check whether any tax-related document needs to be saved

## Internal Quarterly Review

Every quarter:

- Review revenue, if any
- Review expenses
- Check estimated tax questions
- Review state filing duties
- Review payroll/officer compensation questions if applicable
- Update CPA notes

## Rule

Deadlines should be confirmed each year using official IRS and state sources.
